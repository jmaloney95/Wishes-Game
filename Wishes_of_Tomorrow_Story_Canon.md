## 1. Logline

Munen has been frozen for a thousand years. Now it's thawing — the Undying Wish
is waking. The player climbs to **Star Summit** chasing a hooded group who got
there first, toward a sleeping Jirachi that nearly ended the world the last time
it granted a wish. They fail. **Act 2 opens in the world that wish remade.**

---

## 2. Core mythology

### The Undying Wish
A thousand years ago **Jirachi** ("the Undying Wish") granted a wish that went
catastrophically wrong. The result: a disaster severe enough that the world had
to be saved by sealing Jirachi in ice atop the mountain. Munen has been frozen
ever since.

### The thaw = the seal weakening
**Not healing — time/energy leaking from the weakening seal.** Good weather for
a bad reason. The player walks this gradient toward Jirachi:
- **Munen:** still mostly frozen, ice cracking.
- **Melting Mile (Route 1):** the clash zone — snow/ice AND cherry blossoms in
  the same space. *Visual signature of the hack.* (Earlier name "Hanafubuki
  Way" parked as alt.)
- **Frostwood:** fully thawed, almost too warm — uneasy comfort.
- **The Torii Route up to Star Summit:** **inverted thaw** — warmer with
  altitude, ending **molten/volcanic** where the seal has fully failed.

### Who sealed Jirachi (LOCKED concept)
Entombed by the **ancestors of the present-day Elite Four** — the only ones
powerful enough to stop it. Revealed late game (leaning **descendants**). The
Oracle is tied to this lineage.

---

## 3. The knowledge gap (storytelling device, both acts)

**Act 1:** almost no one understands the thaw — only **Professor Clarkson**,
who has read the history. A town reacting to one event in different ways tells
the story with no exposition.

| Group | Reaction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elderly | Dread | They remember the old warnings. |
| Middle-aged | Confusion | They've known nothing but cold. |
| Children | Excitement | They can finally play outside. |

**Act 2 (inverted — LOCKED):** the wish rewrote reality and folded everyone into
it as "normal." **Only the player (and a handful) remember the world before**
(they were inside Jirachi's dream when the wish landed — see §6.7). Now the
player is the one who knows the *world itself* is wrong.

## 4. Key characters

### Professor Clarkson (LOCKED — dies at the summit)
- The Professor; has read the history, **already suspects Jirachi** pre-game.
- His absence (gone up the mountain) is the player's first hook.
- After the Ember Badge, **calls** to congratulate the player and ask them to
  **meet him at Star Summit** to see his discovery.
- **Murdered at the summit:** a masked goon pushes him into the lava. **Last
  words: "destroy my lab and my research."** His death ends Act 1 and his dying
  request becomes the player's first Act 2 quest.

### The Oracle (LOCKED — now battles)
- Formerly "the vanishing old woman." Appears at thresholds; vanishes when
  sought. Munen (warning) → Frostwood (cryptic Jirachi warning) → **the Torii
  Route, where she BATTLES the player (Double Battle, Ghost-types), delivers her
  final Act 1 warning, and vanishes.**
- Tied to the **sealer / Elite Four lineage** (unrevealed in Act 1).
- Tone line: *"You feel the warmth too. Good. The dead feel nothing."*

### The rival — Draco (LOCKED)
- Friendly rival in Munen → **fair mid-route fight on the Torii Route** (basic
  team, player wins) → he **beats the player to Star Summit**; the player
  follows him in and finds him standing with Team Mutrid (affiliation revealed).
- Act 2: the leader's lieutenant; candidate for a **redemption fork** (he too
  half-remembers the old world).

### The fisherman kid (LOCKED)
- Points to **Munen Lake's** frozen far side → future **Red Gyarados** + his
  **missing dad** (Surf-gated Act 2 return).

### Family web (LOCKED — three generations)
- **The Ashlands grandfather** — guides the player in Act 2 (§6.7).
- **Professor Clarkson (father)** — murdered at the summit by his own son.
- **Team Mutrid's leader (grandson)** — **is the masked goon who pushed Clarkson
  into the lava.** He killed his father to seize Jirachi.

Tree: **grandfather → Clarkson (dead) → Mutrid leader.** The man guiding the
player through the ruined Ashlands is the grandfather of the man who unmade the
world and murdered his own father. **The emotional spine of Act 2.**

## 5. The villain group — Team Mutrid (LOCKED)

- **Name:** **Team Mutrid** (reads "putrid / mutated / mutiny").
- **Look:** masked / hooded, **oni-like** — horned masks, dark robes. Strong
  GBA silhouette.
- **Act 1 goal:** claim the Undying Wish. They succeed at the summit — **they
  take Jirachi.**
- **Act 2:** they are **the ruling regime of the region**, installed by the
  wish itself, capital at **Shin-Tokyo** (§6.9).

## 6.5 Frostwood — Onsen / Gym Town  (⚠️ name CONFIRM)

**Role:** first gym + Ember Badge, comic-relief town, second Oracle appearance,
the Cut → Munen Lake → Gyarados loop, and the trigger for the Sennen Line.

**Vibe:** the first genuinely *comfortable* place — comfort that should feel
slightly wrong, because the warmth is. Onsen town at the mountain's base; lake
open, snow soft, everyone treating it like an unexpected vacation. The lighter
the town, the heavier the thaw's true cost lands.

**Visual:** central wooden bathhouse with steam; rotenburo with lounging NPCs;
stone lanterns, bamboo fencing, noren curtains; melting-tundra dressing (slushy
roof snow, meltwater channels, first cherry trees through patchy snow).

**Townsfolk (goofballs):** loungers who won't leave the water; a guy tanning in
the snow; kids in the lake; **one genuinely happy farmer** (best crop year of
his life — the quiet gut-punch); uneasy outliers who saw the oni figures.

### Munen Lake (adjacent — LOCKED)
- Thawed near shore (Cut-gated **Magikarp / Gyarados ~Lv 25**); **frozen far
  side** holds **Red Gyarados + the missing dad** — Surf/flag-gated Act 2
  return.

### Gym Leader — Red Fatality (LOCKED name)
Serious big-fish-in-a-small-pond; the one cocky man in a town of goofballs.
**Insufferably proud** of his recently-caught Fire type he thinks is unique —
the joke being it's an Arcanine (genuinely strong, so he's not *wrong* to be
cocky, just wrong that it's one of a kind). The over-the-top name is part of the
bit; he styles himself a legend.

| Slot | Pokémon | Type | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sneasel | Dark/Ice | fast, punishes underleveled teams |
| 2 | Dewgong **or** Cloyster | Ice/Water | bulky wall |
| 3 (ace) | **Arcanine** | Fire | the "legendary beast" he won't shut up about |

**Difficulty:** harder than a standard first gym; player is **expected to lose
attempt one** — that loss sends them to the Cut sidequest and the lake.
**Reward:** **Ember Badge** + TM (TBD).

### Reactivating the Sennen Line (LOCKED)
On winning the Ember Badge, Red Fatality says he'll **get the Sennen Line train
running again** (Arcanine's fire clears the sabotaged, iced gears). **Off-screen
— no cinematic.** It pays off in Act 1 (opens the ascent) and becomes an **Act 2
travel system** (§6.9).

### Clarkson's call (LOCKED)
Immediately after the badge, Clarkson calls: congratulations, then asks the
player to **meet him at Star Summit.** Personalizes the climb so the summit
death lands harder.

### Progression loop (LOCKED)
Lose to Red Fatality → realize you need a Water answer → wooden-sword kid →
learn **Cut / HM01** → Munen Lake → **Gyarados (~Lv 25)** → beat Red Fatality →
Ember Badge → Sennen Line reactivates → ascent opens. **First time the game
teaches that side content is mandatory to progress.**

## 6.55 Sennen — The Sennen Line Station Town

**Role:** the **gate to the summit** and home of the **Sennen Line** railway.

- The town built around the **Sennen Line station**, long **dormant** (a modern
  line stopped by the freeze, relevant again only now).
- **Sabotage (LOCKED):** a **Team Mutrid goon** has been **icing the gears** to
  keep it dead so no one reaches Jirachi first. The player **battles the goon**
  (sabotage reveal).
- **Reactivation (LOCKED):** with Red Fatality's off-screen thaw + the saboteur
  removed, the line runs and the **Torii Route (Route 3) up to Star Summit
  opens.**
- **Act 2 (LOCKED):** once running, the Sennen Line becomes the region's
  **transit spine** — and in Act 2 it is **Mutrid-controlled** (§6.7).

> Timeline: an inactive modern rail line + Shin-Tokyo supports the region as
> **"modern, stopped by the 1000-year freeze, now restarting."** Committed.

## 6.6 The Torii Route & Star Summit (Act 1 finale)

**Core hook — inverted thaw (LOCKED):** warmer the higher you climb, ending
**molten** at Star Summit, because you approach the seal-energy source.

- **Lower (still cold):** pine forest, patchy snow; hiker trainers; a ranger
  fleeing down who won't go back up.
- **Mid (wrongness):** snow → grass and flowers; birdsong in winter. **First
  Team Mutrid grunts.** **THE ORACLE BATTLE** (Double, Ghost-types) → final
  warning → vanishes.
- **Summit approach (molten):** steam, glowing flowers, then bare igneous heat.
  **Rest area** (shrine/hut: heal/save before the climax). **Two more difficult
  battles.** **DRACO fight** at the last step — player wins, Draco sprints ahead
  and beats them to the top; player follows in.

### Star Summit (LOCKED)
- Open crater, **a lake of lava**, **Jirachi hovering above it, awakened.**
  Mutrid gathered; Draco among them.
- **A masked goon shoves Professor Clarkson into the lava.** Last words:
  **"destroy my lab and my research."**
- **The pusher reveals he is Clarkson's son** (= the leader / grandson; full
  bloodline connects in Act 2). In the moment it lands as: this masked man just
  murdered his own father in front of you.
- **Impossible (unwinnable) battle** → loss is the point.
- **Team Mutrid takes Jirachi.** Hard cut. **End of Act 1.**

## 6.7 Act 2 Opening — The Dream, the Ashlands & the Race Home (LOCKED)

### The wish (LOCKED premise)
At Star Summit, Mutrid's leader used Jirachi to make his wish. It **rewrote the
region:**
- **Turned the ground around the summit to dust** → the **Ashlands** (the wish's
  scar / blast radius).
- **Installed Team Mutrid as the region's rulers** — by the wish, so the world
  treats their rule as the established order.
- **Raised Shin-Tokyo** in the east — Mutrid's gleaming capital, run as a
  **dictatorship** (§6.9). The "tomorrow" they wished for.

### The player remembers (LOCKED)
The player was inside **Jirachi's dream** (a hot spring, dreamlike state) when
the wish landed, so they **wake remembering the world before** while everyone
else accepts the new reality as normal. (Inverts the §3 knowledge gap.)

### The return world (LOCKED — for redesign)
On the way home the player finds the old world **overrun by Mutrid:**
- **Sennen, the Sennen Line train, and Frostwood are MUTRID-OVERRUN** — to be
  **redesigned** as occupied/regime spaces (checkpoints, propaganda, the line
  now a tool of control).
- **Munen is NOT yet reached — preserved exactly as at game start.** But
  **Munen has only one exit (Melting Mile)**, so Mutrid's takeover is
  **imminent.** The player's **family** and **Clarkson's research** are at huge
  risk. *This is the urgency engine of early Act 2: get there first.*

### Opening quest — "Going Home" (LOCKED spine, retraces Act 1 in reverse)
1. **Wake in the Ashlands.** The **grandfather NPC** explains: Team Mutrid did
   this; their leader is his grandson — and **Clarkson's son.** He becomes the
   player's first guide (replacing the dead Professor as mentor).
2. **Descend to Tradewind Town** (§6.8) — the freest place left.
3. **Board the Mutrid-controlled Sennen Line → ride to Sennen.**
4. **Sennen → Route 2 → Frostwood → Melting Mile → Munen**, every town
   recolored by the wish, racing Mutrid down Munen's one road.
5. **Reach Munen, find Clarkson's research before Mutrid does** (§6.9).

This walk-home-through-ruin reuses Act 1 maps re-dressed — cheap to build, heavy
to feel.

## 6.8 Tradewind Town — Act 2 hub (LOCKED role)

**Role:** Act 2 **central hub.** A merchant city on the old trade road that
thrived *because* Munen was frozen and traders routed around it for 1000 years.
Busy, layered Japanese architecture from many eras; fast-traveling news.

**Three jobs:**
1. **Hub** — central map, multiple exits.
2. **Intel** — news arrives first; the player learns where Mutrid moved via
   gossip, informants, rumors. **The theory-spouting NPCs live here** — some
   half-remember the old world, a few genuinely know Jirachi lore.
3. **Recovery / resistance seed** — base camp; the freest town and the natural
   cradle of a resistance.

**Third faction — merchants (LOCKED):** some **profit from the chaos** — sell
Jirachi intel, hawk fake "wish charms," broker with Mutrid for the right price.
Opportunistic, not evil; opens morally-gray sidequests.

**Lore drops (story-through-world):** a traveling storyteller telling the
1000-year tale differently than Munen did; a wish-charm merchant; an old map
showing unreached places; a wanted poster for Team Mutrid; a faded mural of
Jirachi's original sealing (figures in **masks** — the sealers — echoing the
Oracle, the Elite Four, and Mutrid's oni masks).

**Rail junction:** anchored on the **Sennen Line** (Tradewind ↔ Sennen ↔
Shin-Tokyo), now Mutrid-controlled — the system the player unlocked in Act 1,
weaponized by the regime, to be taken back. (Deoxys/meteor blockage CUT.)

## 6.9 Act 2 spine — the counter-wish (LOCKED direction; details TBD)

### Clarkson's research — the central choice (LOCKED)
His life's work = the true history of Jirachi, **how the original sealers bound
it**, and the theory of **how a granted wish can be unmade.** It is the
counter-weapon, which is why his son wants it gone and why his dying word was
*destroy it.*

**The fork — likely a literal in-game choice (LOCKED concept):** when the player
finds the research,
- **Destroy it** (honor Clarkson) — Mutrid can never use it; neither can you;
  forces a harder path to Jirachi. → a purer ending branch.
- **Defy him and use it** — the key to undoing the wish, but it marks you and
  **risks making you what Mutrid is** (someone who'd bend the wish).

Considering making this a **physical choice the player commits to in the game**,
with downstream consequences/endings.

### Mid-game (PROPOSED)
Work east along the Sennen Line toward Shin-Tokyo, gathering what's needed to
reach and counter Jirachi: find the **Elite Four = sealers' heirs** (scattered
under Mutrid's rule; they know the binding method); pay off the **Oracle's**
identity (sealer lineage); **resistance gyms vs. regime-enforcer gyms** (reuses
the counter-trainer design + Ashlands encounter table); **Draco's** redemption
fork. Reclaiming the Sennen Line from the regime is a through-line.

### Shin-Tokyo — the endgame (LOCKED identity)
Eastern terminus; everything frozen and rustic inverted into **neon, concrete,
and surveillance.** **Dictatorship texture:** leader-propaganda murals, an
all-seeing-eye motif, staged-perfect streets over frightened people, a
"Tomorrow is guaranteed" slogan twisting the title. Holds the final gym(s) /
Elite Four, Mutrid's tower, **Jirachi**, and **the grandson.** The
three-generation confrontation (grandfather, player, grandson, the dead father
between them) is the emotional peak.

### The climax & endings (LOCKED moral core)
The player reaches Jirachi; the wish can be touched again (the research +
sealers' method enable it). **Undoing the wish causes everyone *born from it* —
including Shin-Tokyo's population — to cease to exist.** This is framed as a
**mercy: putting them out of their misery**, because the wish's "perfect
tomorrow" is a prison. That is the moral weight of the ending. (Other endings —
re-wish, re-seal/destroy Jirachi — branch off the §6.9 research choice. Final
ending set TBD.)

## 7. Recurring design patterns
1. **The thaw gradient** — warmer/stranger toward Jirachi (Act 1); end-state is
   Shin-Tokyo's manufactured "tomorrow" (Act 2).
2. **"Later" spots** — see-but-can't-reach (Munen Lake's frozen far side first).
3. **Story-through-world, not dialogue** — thaw, weather clash, NPC one-liners,
   always-ahead villains, and **the recolored return world** in Act 2.
4. **The knowledge gap** — Clarkson alone knew (Act 1); the player alone
   remembers (Act 2).
