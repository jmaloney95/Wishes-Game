# Wishes of Tomorrow v1.1.0

**Save-compatible with v1.0.0** — patch a fresh base ROM with this version and keep playing on your existing save.

## New

- **Name your character.** New Game now opens the naming screen instead of a fixed name.
- **Custom battle backdrops across the region** — the open sea on the archipelago routes (with day, dusk and night variants), the Munen Tunnel cavern, the Ashlands savanna, Tradewind's streets and gym, Celebi Island, and dedicated Lab and Space backdrops for the story's biggest fights. Art by carchagui and aveontrainer, credited in-game.
- **Post-game FLY covers the whole region.** Once the ending hands you your ride, every area on the map is a destination — including the islands.
- The Minister has a new lead for anyone who finished his favor.

## Fixed

- Shadow Pokémon battle sprites sat up to 14 pixels too low for 27 species (most visibly on water encounters). All now stand on their proper ground line.
- Surfing south from Shin-Tokyo and returning north from Celebi Island both landed outside Route 129's playable water.
- The tower's street exit dropped you at a stale spot in the city; the command-floor lift arrived too low to see what's waiting on the roof.
- The start menu's log entry no longer overflows, and the redundant EXIT row is gone.
