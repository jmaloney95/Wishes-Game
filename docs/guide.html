FIELD GUIDE

Every map, and the way through it.

The route is the line. The numbers are what is worth stopping for — people to talk to, items on the floor, the odd thing that is easy to walk straight past. Spoilers ahead, obviously.

Act OneAct TwoAct Three
ACT ONE

Act One

Munen Village to the Torii — the region before it turns.

1 — Munen Village

  1. The OracleA foreshadow for what's to come.
  2. Fisherman's sonQuest: The One That Got Away Select QUESTS in the start menu for more details. Hint: Discover the secrets of Munen Lake
  3. Choose StarterGible / Frigibax / Axew
  4. Fisherman returnedOffers to trade his egg for Red Gyarados.
  5. Developer's GiftOn 2F of Player's home, accept the Developer's Gift to receive, Armored Mewtwo LV80

2 — Melting Mile

  1. Fourth StarterInteract to bring Pikachu on your journey.
  2. Toasty timeThe Azurill Girls will block the path to Munen Lake until a key item is obtained.

3 — Walnut Woods

  1. Professor ClarksonLearn some backstory about the region's professor.
  2. Healing / StorageChansey / Porygon serve as the mobile pokemon center.
  3. ItemTM Protect
  4. ItemTM Blizzard - Requires Cut, obtained in National Park
  5. Shortcut / ItemNational Park links back to here for conveniency. TM is Rock Smash.

4 — Frostwood Town

  1. MartPurchase Poke Balls, Potions, Repels
  2. Toasty TimeObtain Hot Cocoa to deliver to the Azurill Girls on Melting Mile. Unlocks access to Munen Lake.
  3. To Route 2Training area for Gym 1. Battles are Lvl 8-12.
  4. Munen Lake OverlookHint at what's hiding beneath the lake.
  5. First GymDefeat 4 trainers to earn the Ember badge. The gym leader, Red Fatality will clear the path through Sennen.
  6. To SennenRecommend visiting before defeating the gym for a piece of storyline with the game antagonist.
  7. Phone callAfter earning the badge, Prof. Clarkson will give you a call to meet him at Star Summit. The path forward is through Sennen.

5 — Munen Lake

  1. PhotographerHints at a Red serpent living beneath the frozen lake
  2. Fishing FailureInteract to receive the Super Rod.
  3. Ice PuzzleGo under the lake to find the Munen kid's lost father, who gets choked up and can't reel in Red Gyarados Lvl20. Receive Ultra Balls x5 from him.

6 — Route 2

  1. BattlesSeveral trainers in this area Lvl8-12 pokemon, including a double battle.
  2. Siren AllisonReturn here later to trigger a Boss battle with Siren Allison with her ace, Mega Lopunny Lvl40.
  3. beach shackAcquire Item Soft Sand
  4. Board the YachtReturn here in the post-game once acquiring the Yacht Key to obtain one of the three Hoenn starters.

7 — Gym 1

  1. Gym LeaderHas Lvl18 Arcanine.

8 — Sennen Line

  1. Shinkansen interiorBefore beating the first Gym, enter the Shinkansen and encounter a Team Mutrid Goon. Defeat him, and he'll freeze the train gears to stop your progress. Red Fatality will use Arcanine to thaw the train allowing access to the National Park.
  2. To National ParkTowards the mountain.

9 — National Park

  1. Park GuideCutscene shows the map, most importantly the gravesite, which will be central to the plot later.
  2. CutReceive the HM Cut. Take the west path back to Walnut Woods to earn TMs Rock Smash & Blizzard
  3. TmTM Earthquake
  4. Eevee TrainerSpend 10,000 for a Shiny Eevee.
  5. Heavy MetalTrade Scyther for one of three random Kanto starters. Scyther can be found in Torii.

10 — Pompeii

  1. mountainside hotelHealing station.
  2. Save FaceSpeak with Master Gen. Retrieve the Carved Mask from Munen Tunnel to receive his reward, badge 2.
  3. Munen TunnelBoulder clears once Save Face quest is started.
  4. Apprentice's HouseThe creator of the Carved Mask has left behind his Diary and a Rare Candy. Access after completing Save Face.
  5. Boulder ObstacleClear the boulders with Rock Smash, which can only be used after earning the badge given by Master Gen.

11 — Munen Tunnel

  1. The Carved MaskDefeat all the goons and the leader to retrieve the Carved Mask.
  2. TMTM Shadow Ball
  3. TMTM Thunderbolt
  4. Northwind CavernThe nest of Shadow Suicune.

12 — Torii

  1. The Oracle ReturnsHer final warning not to ascend the mountain is a Ghost type double battle.
  2. HealingDefeat the Fighting type trainer and your party will be healed.
  3. Return Post GameCome back when Surf is learned to gain access to the area below.
  4. Defeat DracoThe rematch with Rival Draco features evolved starters and is the final test before the showdown at the summit.
ACT TWO

Act Two

The Ashlands, Tradewind, and the Distortion World beneath the park.

1 — Hotsprings

  1. Fugue StateAfter an impossible battle at Star Summit we wake up in a dreamlike state. Stepping out of the spring will spawn you right back in the center. Use the ladder to exit.

2 — Ashlands

  1. Main QuestInteract with the man to learn he is Prof. Clarkson's father, making him The Oni's grandfather. The first of his 3-stage quest is to retrieve the item (2) to help ease him to his rest.
  2. Quest ItemReturn the quest item to the Grandfather to hear his dying wish.
  3. Catalpa BowHe fades to dust, leaving you as the only one capable to fulfill the prophecy. Enter his home and retrieve the Catalpa Bow. The Bow allows communication with the dead, and must be brought to the gravesite in National Park to reconnect with Prof. Clarkson (deceased).
  4. To Ashlands DesertYou can only advance once the Catalpa Bow has been acquired.

3 — Ashlands Desert

  1. MirageWhat appears to be a mirage is actually Shadow Jirachi floating through the desert, foreshadowing The Oni's sinister plans with Jirachi's power.

4 — Tradewind Town

  1. Curio MerchantSells Curios for 1,000 / each. After defeating the Tradewind Gym Leader, Curios can be exchanged for their corresponding Mega Stones.
  2. PC / Department StoreHeal, Box, & 4 floors of wares to purchase.
  3. Tradewind GymGhost type. Up to Lvl40. Recommend moving to the next area then coming back.
  4. Cross the veilSilent boatman will take you to and from National Park (annexed).
  5. Apartment SecretEnter the hidden staircase behind the bookcase to find an underground lab, awarding Mega Stones for all 3 Kanto starters.

5 — Tradewind Apartment

Route only — no marked stops on this map.

6 — Dept. Store 1F

  1. Chansey / PorygonHealing / Box Storage

6 — Dept. Store 2F

  1. Waster BallPurchase a 'Master Ball' for 5,000 from here to discover that it's actually 'Waster Ball' - a complete waste!

6 — Dept. Store 3F

  1. Paw VendorPurchase the 'Mankey's Paw' - new custom item: "A wish: + to one stat, A curse: -1 to another."
  2. vendorPurchase Evolution Stones, Mints, and other standard items

6 — Dept. Store 4F

  1. AM VendorPurchase 'Ability Machines' to customize a Pokemon's ability from one of 20 brand new abilities. Defensive AM's/TM's
  2. AM VendorPurchase Offensive AM's & Offensive TM's

7 — Tradewind Gym

  1. Madame TsujiMega Sableye Lvl40. Awards the Lantern badge & Mega Ring. Enables the Curio Exchange.

8 — National Park (Annexed)

  1. GravesitePlay the Catalpa Bow at the gravesite to enter the Distortion World, and complete this quest.
  2. No escapeYou cannot exit this map. All exit points are guarded by Mutrid Officers.
  3. The 4th BadgeUse the Catalpa Bow to travel deeper and deeper to obtain, the Distortion Badge. Use the Bow again to Teleport home to Clarkson's Lab. This is the point of no return, so collect what you'll need from the area.

9 — Distortion World

  1. GengarProf. Clarkson (deceased) has taken the form of Gengar and joins your party (or box if full). This interaction collapses the Distortion World.

9 — Distortion World L1

  1. Stepping StoneRe-entering the Distortion World takes us here, where a floating stepping stone has appeared. Jump across it to enter the cave.
  2. Rare CandyItem - Rare Candy

9 — Distortion World L2

  1. ItemItem - Max Revive
  2. Mega StoneYour starter's Mega Stone. If you picked Axew, it's Gyaradosite.
  3. TMTM26 - Earthquake
  4. Rift ShardObtain this to collapse the Distortion World and gain passage to the next layer.

9 — Distortion World L3

  1. Mega stoneBaxcalibrite
  2. TMTM25 Thunder
  3. Rift CoreGrab this to collapse the Distortion World and go even deeper into the realm on your next run.

9 — Distortion World L4

  1. ItemPP Up
  2. ItemFull Restore
  3. Mega StoneGarchompite
  4. Rift HeartDefeat Vesper to earn, the Distortion Badge, badge 4.

10 — Munen Lab (Annexed)

  1. The ResearchDefeat Draco, then pick up Prof. Clarkson's research on Shadow Pokemon and Celebi's Purification ability. Choose whether to Keep or Destroy it. Keeping it allows you to Destroy it from the bag later.

11 — Munen Village (Annexed)

Route only — no marked stops on this map.

12 — Rebel Base

  1. The MinisterHe gives you the Shinkansen Key - you'll need it to board the train to Shin-Tokyo.
  2. The Battle for SennenThe plan is to take a Final Stand at the train station and fight back against the Mutrid Officers, while creating a distraction for you to board the train.

13 — Sennen (Annexed)

  1. Face the ApprenticeMaster Gen's former student, Ryoko has turned to the darkside and he stands between us and Shin-Tokyo. Close his storyline.
  2. Boss FightGeneral Edwards deploys from a fighter jet straight down to the ground and flashes his Boss card. Defeat his Mega Tyranitar to board the Shinkansen.

14 — Shinkansen Interior (Annexed)

  1. The next station isShin-Tokyo. Fade to black, Act III: The Infiltration.
ACT THREE

Act Three

Shin-Tokyo, the islands, and the climb up Mutrid Tower.

1 — Shin-Tokyo Station

  1. No escapeCannot return to prior areas via train.

2 — Shin-Tokyo

  1. Quest TriggerReceive a call from The Minister. Mikmanc has been captured.
  2. Quest UpdateThe guards leave the site and leave the jail entrance open for you to enter.
  3. CapturedPost-jailbreak you are surrounded by Mutrid Goons and The Oni himself escorts you personally.
  4. Rebel HideoutNessa and Zoroark reveal their disguise as The Oni and take you to their Rebel HQ. This is your home base for Act III and you will respawn here.
  5. Harness DarknessYou are Wanted for treason against The Oni for breaking out Mikmanc. Mutrid Officers will challenge you to battle using Shadow Pokemon. Capture one using the Snag Machine.
  6. The Shadow ResearchThe pool of Shadow species varies depending on if you Kept or Destroyed the Research. If the Research was taken upon entry to Shin-Tokyo, The Oni has accelerated Shadow production and Pokemon found will be much stronger.
  7. Tides Run LowAfter Quest: Jailbreak is completed, the areas around Shin-Tokyo are unlocked. Surf south to find Celebi Island and purify the Shadow Pokemon you've caught.
  8. The TowerThe Tower is the "Elite Four". The door is locked until you purify a Shadow Pokemon.
  9. The docksThe Minister remains at the docks and gives the reward for Jailbreaker. He also runs the Mega Stone exchange. 10,000 for the stone of your lead Pokemon.
  10. Tokyo UndergroundUnderground gym in Shin-Tokyo. Earn the 5th badge here.
  11. Street VendorsPurchase items from Tradewind Dept. Store: Ability Machines (AMs), Paws, and every type of Poke Ball including Snag Balls. You can snag Shadows once the Snag Machine is obtained in the Jail, but Snag Balls have increased Shadow catch rates.
  12. CasinoStandard gambling games with custom prizes. Coin Case can be found in a trash can here.
  13. East EntranceEnter the station from here. Quick access back to West entrance.
  14. PCStandard Pokemon Center.
  15. Armored MewtwoAfter the credits roll, you get a call to meet Draco by the docks. He waits here with Armored Mewtwo as a reward for defeating him.

3 — Shin-Tokyo Jail

  1. StealthMake your way to the cells to break out Mikmanc with the lights out. Tip: if you wear the Carved Mask, the first 3 Mutrid Officers won't challenge you to battle.
  2. Rescue MikmancThe character from Gym 1 returns, and rescuing him rewards, The Snag Machine, giving the ability to catch Shadow Pokemon.
  3. ItemBreak out the other prisoner to receive Quick Claw.
  4. escort MikmancEscape out the West exit, facing two Shadow trainers on your way out.

4 — Rebel Hideout

  1. NessaThis is your Home Base in Shin-Tokyo. Nessa and Zoroark, disguised as The Oni reveals their true form.
  2. Chansey / PorygonHeal & box
  3. Prof. AssistantHe worked for The Oni and tells details about the Shadow Generator he was building to brainwash Pokemon and people. Show him a Purified Pokemon to redeem this Quest. Then show him 5 in total.

5 — Surf Route 1

Route only — no marked stops on this map.

6 — Celebi Island

  1. The keepersDefeat the three trainers to unlock access to Celebi's Purification Chamber.
  2. The guardiansAzelf, Uxie & Mesprit guard the chamber until they deem you worthy by defeating The Keepers.
  3. celebiUnlocking allows you to present any Shadow Pokemon in your party to Celebi. To Purify, you must first "open their heart" by bringing them into battle. Their shadow icon will turn purple when they are ready to purify.
  4. to prototype islandSurf North to reach Prototype Island. The 'Archipelago Map' outlines the locations of the 3 islands. Once received, open Region Map > Press R.

7a — Prototype Island

  1. To Prototype LabGo downstairs to The Oni's original Lab where the failed prototype for the Shadow Generator still remains.
  2. To Ryuden IslandSurf north to reach Ryuden Island

7b — Prototype Lab

  1. Shadow DeoxysBattle Shadow Deoxys Lvl50. Run from battle or KO, and it stays here until captured.

8 — Surf Route 2

Route only — no marked stops on this map.

9 — Ryuden Island

  1. Dragon keepersInteract with the eggs to initiate a challenging double battle featuring high level Dragon-type Pokemon.
  2. RewardDefeat the Dragon Keepers to earn an egg containing one of 12 random dragons.

10 — Shin-Tokyo Underground

  1. The AnnouncerSpeak to the announcer to enter into the Gauntlet consisting of back to back battles without healing breaks.
  2. Gym 5Step onto the platform to initiate the Gauntlet. Three battles including Leader Raiden, and his Lvl50 Electric types.

11 — Tokyo Tower Street

  1. Street LevelWhite Tiger (Byakko): Incineroar, Chien Pao, Mega Absol, Landorus Therian, Meowscarada, Shadow Raikou

12 — Tokyo Tower Mid

  1. Tower MidVermillion Bird (Suzaku): Talonflame, Mega Altaria, Fezandipiti, Blaziken, Galarian Moltres, Shadow Ho-Oh

13 — Tower Command

  1. Tower CommandAzure Dragon (Seiryu): Mega Gyarados, Dragonaire (With Eviolite), Dragapult, Walking Wake, Latios, Miraidon
  2. Final BadgeDefeat the commander to receive, the Tower badge, and the Master Ball.

14 — Tower Final Boss

  1. The OniThe final encounter of the Main Story and first time seeing The Oni since he KO'd the Professor on Star Summit. Defeat him and Destroy the Shadow Generator.
  2. Shadow JirachiAfter The Oni dolphin-dives off the tower into his plane, Shadow Jirachi is the only thing keeping the Shadow Generator running. Capture it to destroy the machine. Catch rate is low, recommend Master ball.
  3. The Shadow GeneratorThe machine used to corrupt Pokemon explodes and all trapped Shadow Legendary Pokemon are scattered across the region.

15 — Post Game Base

  1. Post-GameAfter the credits roll, continuing the story brings you back to the Rebel Hideout, along with some familiar faces from throughout the game.
  2. PJAs a reward, Nessa gives the Private Jet, and essentially, HM Fly, to navigate to any visited area of the region.
  3. What nowFinish the plot with Rival Draco, Sail from Shin-Tokyo to the Battle Frontier, Complete the Shadow Log.
