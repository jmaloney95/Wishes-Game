## About this hack

### What is Pokémon Wishes of Tomorrow?

**Wishes of Tomorrow** is a full-length ROM hack of *Pokémon Emerald* with an original region, an original cast, and a three-act story that actually finishes — ending, credits, Hall of Fame, post-game and all. It isn't a patched binary: it's built from source on RHH's `pokeemerald-expansion`, so every map, system and asset compiles from scratch — 64 new maps, 34 new tilesets and a custom soundtrack in a 32 MB cartridge.

It starts in Munen Village, a mountain shrine town under Mt. Fuji, where the thousand-year ice on the peak is breaking. It ends somewhere much darker.

### About the game

**The conflict**

You lose in Act II. Team Mutrid breaks the seal on Mt. Munen and the region you spent Act I walking through comes apart — towns occupied, allies scattered, your own hometown turned into a checkpoint. Act I's quiet mountain journey is the setup for that.

Act III is the rebellion. A train drops you in Shin-Tokyo, Mutrid's neon capital, with a Snag Machine and a bounty on a building-sized screen. The surviving gym leaders are in exile on the archipelago offshore, and every badge you take back is one more of them joining the assault on the tower.

**The Shadow Pokémon system**

- Shadow Pokémon hit harder, defend worse, can snap into Hyper Mode — and gain no experience at all until they're purified
- **Snag Balls** steal a Shadow straight out of a trainer battle
- Battle a snagged Shadow once to open it, then purify it at the Celebi Island shrine
- A **Shadow Log** tracks every Shadow in the game as loose, snagged or purified

**Choices that carry**

- What you do with Clarkson's research at the end of Act II is a real fork. Destroy it and Mutrid's Act III patrols stay capped. Keep it and their Shadow pool escalates hard — Haxorus, Melmetal, Ursaluna, Rhyperior, Annihilape — and you're the only one who gets to read the dossier. Neither path locks content

**Quality of life**

- Quest journal in the START menu — 12 questlines, live objectives, on-screen updates
- HM field-move menu — no HM slave, no wasted party slot
- Fast travel and custom heal points across the region
- Searchable bag
- Character portraits during dialogue
- Animated boss intro cards and scripted set-pieces
- Curio shop for Mega Stones
- Ability Machines
- Modern battle engine via `pokeemerald-expansion` 1.15.3 — current species, moves, abilities and mechanics

### New 1.2.0 features

**Save-compatible with 1.1.0 and 1.0.0** — patch a fresh base ROM and keep playing your existing file. (Randomizer Mode is chosen at new game, so a randomized run needs a new save.)

- **Randomizer Mode** — Gen 1–9 base forms only
- **A new starter trio** — Gible, Frigibax and Axew
- **A post-game region chart for the archipelago** — press R on the map to toggle mainland and islands
- **The Battle Frontier is reachable** — after the ending, the Shin-Tokyo ferry runs to it
- **The Game Corner has real prizes** — fifteen Pokémon plus thirteen TMs and evolution items

### How to play

Legally obtain a **Pokémon Emerald (U)** ROM — 16 MB.

1. Grab the latest `wishes-of-tomorrow` from the releases page
2. Apply it with the in-browser patcher at [flatfootlabs.com](https://flatfootlabs.com/) — or with any BPS patcher (Flips, Rom Patcher JS)
3. Play the 32 MB output in mGBA or another emulator. flatfootlabs.com will also just run it in your browser

There's a full Player's Guide for Acts I–III if you get properly stuck.

### Future Plans

The main campaign is content complete — the work from here is the post-game.

Freeing what's in the tower scattered Shadow legendaries across the region, and I want that hunt to be a real chapter rather than a checklist: more of the deeper archipelago opened up, the remaining islands built out, and more challenging battles.
