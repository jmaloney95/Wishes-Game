### 2026-08-28 (round 18) — title screen sharpening: dither out, canvas grid out (BUILT green 02:34, untested)
- **THE "FLICKERING PIXELS" WERE FLOYD-STEINBERG DITHER.** Joe reported pixels flickering on the Pokemon logo and in the rocks. Dither fakes a third colour by chequering two others; on a STATIC screen that chequer reads as shimmer, and it is also why nothing looked solid. **Quantising with `dither=Image.NONE`** fixed the logo, the rocks and the flatness complaint in one move -- with 255 colours available there is nothing to fake.
- **THE CANVAS GRID TOOK FOUR ATTEMPTS. Three plausible models were all wrong:**
  1. *A dark line every 21 source px.* No -- 21 is FOUR lines. The true period is one drawn pixel: 1259/240 = **5.2458 source px**.
  2. *Repaint the darkest line, phase found from a global profile.* The phase came out wrong for rows because the picture's own content dominates the fold; half the mesh survived.
  3. *The lines sit on cell boundaries, so sample cell interiors.* **Measured, the darkest phase is 44% INTO the cell -- near its middle.** Interior sampling was aiming directly at the grid, which is why it scored WORSE on a 4-periodic test than plain LANCZOS.
- **What actually worked: a PER-CELL MEDIAN.** Each drawn pixel is a 5.25 x 5.25 block of source pixels and the grid crosses about one row and one column of it -- a **minority** of the samples, which a median discards by construction. No period, phase, colour or opacity has to be guessed correctly. It also returns a flat block exactly (solid colours) and does no cross-cell blending (sharp), so it answered all three of Joe's complaints at once. The median is then **snapped to a colour that genuinely occurs in the block**, so every output pixel is a colour the artist really used rather than a per-channel blend of three.
- **RULE worth keeping: to recover pixel art from an upscaled/JPEG'd export, take a per-cell MEDIAN, not a resample.** Averaging filters rake every contaminant -- grid lines, ringing, JPEG mosquito noise -- into every output pixel; a median rejects anything that is not the majority of the cell. The same idea is already in the Clarkson portrait pipeline ("median native extract") and in Allison's 2x halving (majority vote per 2x2).
- A measurement caveat that cost time: **a 4-periodic amplitude test cannot tell grid from art.** Sharper sampling preserves more real high-frequency detail and therefore scores higher, so the metric ranked the best method last. Judged by eye after that.
- TEST CHECKLIST: logo and rocks are flat colour with no shimmer; no mesh in the sky; edges crisp; PRESS START / v1.2.0 still blink together and sit exactly over their painted-out patch.
